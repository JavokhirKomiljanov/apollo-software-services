FAQ

Questions we get on the first call

Where is the team?

Apollo is a US-facing studio with a senior engineering bench that works 4–8 hours of overlap with the East Coast every working day. You get one point of contact, in English, and an NDA from day one.

Why is a free mockup possible?

Because we only take on work we can scope in an hour. A one-page site or booking flow takes us a day to preview — cheaper than a sales meeting, and you see the real thing instead of a slide deck.

What is not in the price?

Hosting and domains, third-party fees (Stripe, SMS, OpenAI), photography and copy you want written from scratch, and anything added after the approved scope — we quote that separately before we do it.

Who owns the site and the accounts?

You do. Domain, hosting, Google Business Profile and booking accounts are opened in your name; we hand over every login at launch.

Can you work with my existing site or booking app?

Yes — we embed Booksy, Fresha, Square, Vagaro, Mindbody and most others, and we fix WordPress, Wix and Squarespace sites rather than insisting on a rebuild.

How do payments work?

Under $3,000: nothing until you approve the mockup, then 50% to start and 50% at launch. Larger builds are paid by milestone. Retainers are monthly, cancel any time.
